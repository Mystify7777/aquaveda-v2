# Parking Lot

Ideas that are plausible, sometimes good, and explicitly not this release.
Goes here instead of into the roadmap or a TODO. Revisit when scoping a
release that's actively looking for new scope.

- Organization accounts (NGOs, government bodies)
- Notification system
- Satellite imagery overlays
- Water quality prediction modeling
- IoT sensor integration
- Gamification (XP, contribution score, badges)
- Offline sync beyond basic PWA caching
- Government / regional dashboards
- NGO partnership tooling
- Heatmap and density analytics beyond marker clustering
- Temporal issue playback (watch status change over time)
- A second domain beyond water
- Hybrid Gemini/LLM recommendation layer (rule engine ships first,
  LLM is additive when explicitly decided)
- Expert application: email notification of decisions (needs provider, secrets, delivery/privacy ownership; the in-app timeline ships first)
- Expert application: withdraw or edit a pending application
- Expert application: re-apply cooldown / abuse limits
- Expert application: supporting document uploads
- Expert application: queue position and applicant activity signals (approved articles, reports filed)
- Remediation (ADR-0007): evidence links/attachments and per-issue-type evidence requirements (before escalation work)
- Remediation (ADR-0007): count *active* experts (not just EXPERT accounts) for the self-grant threshold
- Remediation (ADR-0007): stored volunteer offers ("offer to help") requiring EXPERT approval
- Remediation (ADR-0007): multiple assignees (needs a grants collection ADR), grant expiry/reminders
- Public activity log: merge comments, Project creation, and escalation/progress events with per-source public projections
- Issue dispositions for invalid/duplicate/withdrawn reports
